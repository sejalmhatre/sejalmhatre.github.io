Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mail Attachment Sync is a private tool used by its owner. This policy explains what it does with data.

What the tool accesses

It reads email attachments from the owner's own Zoho Mail account and uploads copies to the owner's own Google Drive. In Google Drive it can only access files and folders that the tool itself created (Google Drive scope: drive.file).

How data is stored

Attachments and their text copies are stored only in the owner's Google Drive. Login tokens are stored only on the computer or server that runs the tool.

Sharing

The tool does not sell, share, publish or transfer data to anyone else. It has no advertising, analytics or third-party services.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can delete the files from Google Drive at any time and can remove the tool's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
